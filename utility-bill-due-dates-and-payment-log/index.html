How it works

  1. Add each bill once: electricity, gas, water, internet, each phone, the building maintenance, and the day it is usually due.
  2. When a bill comes, type the amount and due date (or tap Same as last bill).
  3. See what needs paying: "Internet 3,499 due Thu (in 2 days)", overdue bills in red, and what is still to pay this month.
  4. Tap Paid with the date and the reference number, so you can prove it was paid. Each bill is compared with last year to spot a jump.

Questions

Does it connect to my electricity or gas company?

No. You type the amount and the due date when the bill comes, and tap Paid when you pay. Nothing is linked to your bank or your provider, so it works for any company in any country.

My bill has a higher amount after the due date

Many bills show "payable after due date". Tap + Add the amount after the due date when you type the bill in. If it is not paid in full by the due date, the app asks for that amount instead.

I paid a bill in two parts

Tap Paid and type the amount you paid. The bill shows what is left; tap Paid again for the rest.

A bill comes every 2 months, or once a year

Choose it under A new bill comes. The next due date is worked out from the last bill.

How is "higher than last year" worked out?

Each bill is compared with the bill due in the same month a year before, so summer is compared with summer. If there is none yet, it is compared with the bill before it.

The bill is paid automatically

Tick Paid automatically for that bill. It is not shown as overdue; after the due date the app asks you to check it was taken, then tap It was taken.

Is it private? Does it work offline?

Everything stays in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, it works with no connection. Save a backup file before changing phones.
