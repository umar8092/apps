          Questions

          How does the tasbeeh after prayer work?

          Pick Tasbeeh after prayer (33, 33, 34) and tap. After 33 SubhanAllah the counter moves to Alhamdulillah, after 33 more to Allahu Akbar, and after 34 one round is done and it starts again. The target is 5 rounds a day, one after each prayer. You can change the phrases, the counts and the rounds with Edit.

          Can I have a target only on Fridays?

          Yes. Add a dhikr such as Durood, set Target each day to 1,000 and choose Only some days and Fri. On other days it still counts, with no target, and your streak only looks at Fridays.

          We are reading 1,25,000 Ayat-e-Karima as a family. Can it help?

          Add a Big goal of 125,000, with the date of the gathering and the number of people. It shows how many each person does (3 people: 2 do 41,667 and 1 does 41,666) and how many a day are still needed. When someone tells you their count, use Change count and Add to today. Copy today's summary gives text for WhatsApp.

          I tapped too many times.

          Tap −1 to take back one. For more, open Change count, type the right number and tap Set today to this.

          When does a new day start?

          At midnight, or at the hour you pick under A new day starts at. With 04:00, dhikr at 01:30 still counts for the day before.

          Does the app give religious rulings?

          No. It only counts. The phrases and numbers are the common ones; ask a scholar about anything else.

          Is it private? Does it work offline?

          Your counts stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Use Save a backup file to move to a new phone. Once loaded, the app works with no connection, even in a mosque with no signal.
