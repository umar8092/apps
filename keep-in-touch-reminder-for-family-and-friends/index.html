Keep in Touch Reminder for Family and Friends

Choose how often you want to be in touch with each person. Tap Called or Messaged after each contact, and see who is overdue and who to call today.

This browser is not letting the app save. What you type will be lost when you close the page. Save a backup file before you leave.

How it works

  1. Add each person and how often you want to be in touch, such as every 3 days for parents or every month for an old friend.
  2. After each call, message or visit, tap Called, Messaged or Met up in their box.
  3. Open the app any day to see who to get in touch with today: “Nani: 2 days overdue, last called 9 days ago”. The most overdue come first.

Questions

How does it work out who is overdue?

It adds how often you want to be in touch to the day you were last in touch. Nani, every week, last called on Sun 18 Oct: due on Sun 25 Oct, so on Tue 27 Oct her box says 2 days overdue. Someone you message every 3 days, last on Sat 24 Oct, is due today. The most overdue person is always at the top.

I called but nobody answered

Tap Tried, no answer. Their box then says when you tried, so you remember to try again, but they stay due until you really talk or message.

I forgot to log a call, or it was days ago

Tap + Another day or a note about it (optional) in their box, pick the day, and then tap Called, Messaged or Met up. You can also add what you talked about. Anything in History can be changed with Edit or removed with Delete, with Undo.

They live abroad. When can I call?

Add their time zone under + Their time zone and good times to call. Their box shows the time where they are now and how far ahead or behind you they are. With good times to call, for example 09:00 to 21:00, it says whether now is a good time, or when the next good time starts in your time. Daylight saving is handled by your device.

We are on holiday together, or they are staying with us

Tap Pause reminders in their box. They move to the end and are not counted as overdue. Tap Resume later: if they would have been overdue, they are due that day, not weeks late.

Can it remind me on my phone?

Tap Add next due day to calendar in a person's box. It saves a calendar file for the day they are due with a reminder at 09:00, which your phone's calendar can open.

Is it private? Does it work offline?

The names and notes stay in this browser on this device: no account, it never reads your contacts, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection. Save a backup file now and then, or before changing phones.
