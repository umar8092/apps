            Questions

            How does it decide who does what?

            Each chore is Light (1 point), Medium (2) or Heavy (3). Every week the jobs, heaviest first, go to whoever has done the fewest points so far, and nobody gets the same job twice in a row when someone else can take it. With 4 people and 4 chores, everyone does every chore once every 4 weeks.

            What if we have more chores than people, or more people than chores?

            Both work. With more chores, people get two or more jobs a week, balanced by points. With more people, someone has a week off now and then, and it is always a different person.

            Someone is away. Who does their jobs?

            Tap Mark away on their name (use After to mark a holiday in later weeks). Their jobs go to the people at home that week, and they do not have to make up for it when they come back.

            Two of us want to swap. Is that unfair later?

            Tap Change next to the job and pick who does it this week. If one person ends up doing more, the rota gives them less in the following weeks, so it evens out.

            What about jobs done every day, like washing up?

            Choose Every day (one person for the whole week). One person does it every day that week, and someone else the next week. Mark it Heavy if it is a lot of work.

            We all use our own phones. How do we share it?

            One person keeps the rota and taps Copy this week or Share this week to post it in the group chat, or prints 4 weeks for the fridge. To move the rota to another phone, use Save a backup file and Restore a backup.

            Is it private? Does it work offline?

            The names and chores stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
