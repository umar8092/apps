          Questions

          How is the monthly total worked out?

          Each subscription is turned into a cost per year (a year counts 12 months, 52 weeks or 365 days), added up, and divided by 12. So a 10.00 weekly gym counts as 43.33 a month. Paused and cancelled subscriptions are not counted.

          What happens when a payment date passes?

          It moves forward to the next payment by itself, so the list is right every time you open it. A payment on the 31st is taken on the last day of shorter months. A free trial whose end date has passed counts as paying.

          Can it remind me before I pay?

          Tap Add payment reminders to my calendar. It saves a calendar file with each payment repeating, a reminder the day before, and a warning 2 days before a free trial ends. Open the file to add it to Google, Apple or Outlook calendar.

          Why add cancelled subscriptions?

          So you can see how much you save each year, and keep a note of what you cancelled and when.

          Is it private?

          Yes. There is no account and no bank link. Your list stays in this browser on this device, and the app works offline once it has loaded.
