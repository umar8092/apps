      Questions

      Will it ring if my phone locks?

      A locked phone stops web pages, so the sound cannot play until you unlock it. That is why the app keeps the screen on while timers run (turn it off under Alarm and screen). When you come back, each timer shows the right time, or says “Done 3 min ago”, and a timer that finished in the last 10 minutes rings straight away.

      I do not hear anything.

      Browsers only play sound after you tap the page; pressing Start timer does that. After a reload, tap Turn sound on. On an iPhone the silent switch also mutes web pages. Press Play under Alarm and screen to test the volume.

      What do Restart and +1 min do?

      Restart starts the timer again from its full length (shown on the button). +1 min adds a minute; on a finished timer it runs one more minute, handy when the pasta needs a little longer.

      Can I use it for things other than cooking?

      Yes: laundry, a bath, screen time, parking, a nap, plant watering. Give each timer a name so you know which one is ringing.

      Is it private? Does it work offline?

      Your timers and saved timers stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection, and you can add it to your home screen.
