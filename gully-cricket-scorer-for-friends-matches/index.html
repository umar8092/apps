            Questions

            How are wides and no balls counted?

            By your house rules. Normally a wide or a no ball gives 1 run and is bowled again, so the over has an extra ball. Choose No run or No, it counts as a ball if your group plays that way. Runs the batters run on a wide go to wides; runs hit off a no ball go to the batter.

            Rain stopped play. Can we cut the overs?

            Yes. Open Match options and change the overs for this innings. In the chase you can also type a new target. If you do not, the target stays the first team's score plus one.

            What does "need 23 off 14 balls" mean?

            The team batting second needs 23 more runs to win, and has 14 legal balls left. The required rate is how many runs per over that is.

            Our teams are uneven. Is that okay?

            Yes. Each team has its own number of players, and a team is all out when one batter is left (or when everyone is out, if the last batter may bat alone). In Each player too, a player can also bat again.

            Is it private?

            Yes. The match is saved only on this device and works offline in a park with no signal. Your data is never sent anywhere.
