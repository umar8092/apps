        Questions
        
        
        How often should I replace a toothbrush, a water filter or contact lenses?
        Toothbrushes and electric brush heads: every 3 months, or sooner if the bristles are frayed or after being ill (NHS and American Dental Association advice). Water filter jug cartridges: usually every 4 weeks. Contact lenses: exactly as your optician says, 2-weekly lenses every 2 weeks and monthly lenses every month, and the lens case every 3 months. The numbers in "Pick a common item" are the usual advice; if the box, your optician or your dentist says something else, change the number.
        
        
        I replaced it early, late, or on another day. What happens?
        Tap Replaced today on the day you change it: the next date always counts from that day. A toothbrush replaced every 3 months and changed on 2 Aug is next due on 2 Nov. Changed it on another day? Tap Edit and set "Last replaced". Months are calendar months: 31 Jan plus 1 month is 28 Feb (29 Feb in a leap year).
        
        
        What does Pause do?
        Use it for something not in use for a while: the AC in winter, contact lenses while you wear glasses on holiday. A paused item never shows as due. When you tap Resume, the paused days are not counted, so a filter used for 2 weeks before a 3-month pause still has the rest of its time left.
        
        
        Will it remind me?
        A web page cannot ring by itself when it is closed, so use Add the due dates to my calendar: your phone's calendar then reminds you at 9:00 on each due date. The app itself shows what is due every time you open it.
        
        
        Is it private? Does it work offline?
        Your list stays in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Use Save a backup file to move it to a new phone. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
