          Questions

          How does it work out the number from dates?

          It counts every day from the first day you missed to the last day, both included. 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2019 is 365 days, so you owe 365 of each prayer: 365 Fajr, 365 Dhuhr and so on. Days you leave out are taken off every prayer.

          I already made some up, or I missed only some prayers.

          Choose Type the numbers and type how many of each prayer are left, such as 1,200 Fajr and 300 Dhuhr. You can change the totals at any time with Change the totals; what you have made up is kept.

          When will I finish?

          Pick your daily goal. With 4,000 days of prayers left and a goal of 2 days of prayers a day, you finish in 2,000 days. Today counts as the first day. The box also shows your real pace over the last 30 days.

          Why is Witr a choice?

          Some people make up Witr and some do not. Untick Count Witr too if you do not. The app does not decide for you.

          Does the app give religious rulings?

          No. It only counts the numbers you enter. Ask a scholar what you owe, how to pray qaza, or about fidya and kaffarah.

          Is it private? Does it work offline?

          Your counts stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Use Save a backup file to move to a new phone. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
