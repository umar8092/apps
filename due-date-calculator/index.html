    Questions

    How is the due date calculated?

    A pregnancy is counted as 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last period. If your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days, the date moves by the difference. From conception it is 266 days. After IVF it is 261 days from a day 5 transfer and 263 days from a day 3 transfer. From a scan, the weeks and days it measured are counted back to day zero.

    Why does it say 5 weeks when I conceived 3 weeks ago?

    Doctors start counting from the first day of your last period, about two weeks before conception. That is why a pregnancy lasts 40 weeks, not 38.
