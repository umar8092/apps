      Questions

      How does it know if sessions will go to waste?

      It divides the sessions left by the days left, today and the last day included, and tells you how often to go: 4 left with 37 days to go is "about 1 every 9 days". After two weeks and at least two sessions, it also looks at your own pace. Used 6 in 25 days? Then about 8 more fit before the end, so you are fine. Used 2 in 30 days with 6 left and 20 days to go? It warns that about 5 could go to waste.

      What is the difference between Went, Missed and Cancelled?

      Went uses one session. Missed (still counted) is for a class you skipped that the centre still takes off your pack. Cancelled or excused (not counted) is for a class the centre cancelled, or one you gave notice for: it is kept in the log but does not use a session. A make-up class is simply Went on the day you go.

      I had already used some sessions before I found the app.

      When you add the pack, tap "+ Already used some?" and type how many. They count as used, and the log says "Used before this log was started".

      Two children share one pack. Can I see who went?

      Yes. Type both names in "Who uses it?", like "Ali, Sara". The pack's box then has a button for each, "Ali went today" and "Sara went today", and shows how many each one used.

      What about a monthly pass with unlimited visits?

      Choose "A pass with unlimited visits" and set its last day. Tap "Went today" each visit: the app counts the visits and shows what each one cost you, so you can see if the pass is worth it.

      The centre says I have a different number left.

      Tap "Copy log" or "Email log" and show them the dated list. If they gave you extra sessions or more time, tap Edit and change the number of sessions or the last day.

      Is it private? Does it work offline?

      Your packs and logs stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
