How it works

  1. Add each thing you are selling with its price and the sites you listed it on.
  2. Note every offer. When a buyer agrees, tap Reserve for a buyer with the price and the day they collect.
  3. When they pay and take it, tap Collected and paid. You see what sold, what is left and the money you made.

    Questions

    When should I lower my price?

    If an item has had the same price for 14 days and nobody has made an offer, it is marked Price drop due and moves to the top of For sale. Many sellers drop about 10% at that point. Tap Change price: the old prices are kept, so you see “Was 15,000, then 13,500, now 12,000”.

    A buyer did not turn up

    Tap Buyer did not come. The item goes back to For sale and the no-show is noted on it, so you remember next time. If they paid a deposit, choose whether you gave it back or kept it; a kept deposit is added to Money made.

    I sold several things together

    Tap Mark as sold on one of them, open + Sold together with other items and tick the others. Type the one price you got: it is split between the items by their asking prices, to the cent. A cot asking 9,000 and a high chair asking 3,000 sold together for 10,000 count as 7,500 and 2,500.

    I swapped it instead of selling it

    In Mark as sold, open + Swapped it for something and type what you got, plus any money (or 0). Swaps are not counted in the “% of what you first asked” figure.

    What does “% of what you first asked” mean?

    It compares what your sold items fetched with the first asking price you gave them. Asking 10,000 and 5,000 and selling for 8,000 and 5,000 shows 87% (13,000 of 15,000). Items with no asking price and swaps are left out.

    Is it private? Does it work offline?

    Your list and photos stay in this browser on this device. No account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection. Save a backup file now and then, or before changing phones.
