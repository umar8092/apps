          Questions

          How is the last day to return worked out?

          The app adds the return days to the day you bought it, or to the delivery day if you add one. Bought on 3 October with 30 days to return, the last day is Monday 2 November. Shops differ, so check the receipt or the shop's returns page, and choose "Until a date" if it gives a fixed date.

          When does a warranty end?

          On the day before the same date at the end of the warranty. Bought on 3 October 2026 with a 2-year warranty, it is covered until 2 October 2028. If that date does not exist, the last day of the month is used (bought 31 January with 1 month, covered until 28 February).

          What is an extended warranty here?

          Cover you bought on top of the maker's warranty, such as 3 more years from the shop. It starts the day after the maker's warranty ends, and the item stays under "Under warranty" until both have ended.

          I decided to keep it. How do I stop the return countdown?

          Tap Keeping it. The item moves to "Under warranty" and the return deadline is left out of reminders. Tap Might return it to bring the countdown back while the window is still open.

          How do the calendar reminders work?

          Calendar on an item, or Add all deadlines to my calendar, saves a calendar file. Open it to add all-day events to your phone's or computer's calendar: the last day to return, with reminders 3 days and 1 day before, and the warranty end, with reminders 30 and 7 days before.

          Something broke. What do I need for a claim?

          Use Find an item, then tap Copy on it. You get the shop, the date, the price, the order and serial numbers and when the warranty ends, ready to paste into an email or a claim form. Tap Receipt to show the receipt photo.

          Is it private? Does it work offline?

          Your purchases and receipt photos stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Use Save a backup file to move them to a new phone. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
