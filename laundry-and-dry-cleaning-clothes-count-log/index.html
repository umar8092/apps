      Questions

      How is the bill worked out?

      Each kind of clothing is its count times its price: 5 shirts × 40 = 200. The lines are added up, plus any extra charge such as Express, minus anything the shop agreed to take off for a lost or damaged piece. Lost pieces are not charged. Everything is counted in whole pieces and whole cents, so the bill is exact.

      Some pieces did not come back

      In the batch, tap + next to each kind as it comes back, or All back when everything is home. The batch then says exactly what is still at the shop, such as Shirt × 2, and for how many days. Set Usually ready in for the shop to see when a batch is late.

      A piece was lost or damaged

      Tap Lost or damaged in the batch. Set how many of each kind are lost (they are not charged), type the amount the shop agreed to pay you or take off the bill, and a note. If the shop owes you more than the bill, the batch says so.

      The shop changed its prices

      Type the new price when you give the next batch. Old batches keep the price they were given at, and the new price is remembered for next time.

      Washing and dry cleaning at the same time?

      Save them as two batches for the same day, one with Wash and iron and one with Dry cleaning. Each service remembers its own prices.

      Is it private? Does it work offline?

      Everything stays in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection. Save a backup file now and then, or before changing phones.
