Bus and Train Timetable for My Stops

Type your stop's timetable once. See when the next bus, train or shuttle leaves, when to set off and when the last one goes.

This browser is not letting the app save. Your timetables will be lost when you close the page. Save a backup file before you leave.

Add a stop and its times

Copy the departure times from the timetable at the stop, the station or the school's or company's shuttle sheet. You only do this once.

How it works

  1. Type the times once for your stop, for example 07:15 07:40 08:05, or paste the column from a timetable. Separate times for weekdays, Saturday and Sunday if they differ.
  2. See the answer at a glance: "Next 07:40, in 7 min, then 08:05 and 08:30".
  3. Add your walk to the stop and it tells you when to leave: "Leave in 2 min to catch the 07:40".
  4. Add the way home as a second stop, so the evening times are ready too, with the last one tonight.

Public holidays

On these dates every stop runs its Sunday times, like most buses and trains.

    Backup

    Save a backup file before changing phones, or to give the same timetables to a friend or your child.

    Questions

    Does the app know where the bus really is?

    No. It shows the times from the timetable you typed, so it works for any town, any company and a school or factory shuttle with no app at all. If a bus runs late or is cancelled, the timetable cannot know that.

    Where do I get the times?

    From the timetable at the stop or the station, the company's website or leaflet, or the shuttle sheet from your school, university or work. Take a photo of it and type the times for your stop, or copy the text from a website and paste it into Departure times: the app picks out the times and skips the words.

    What about buses after midnight?

    Type them in order after the evening times, like 23:40 00:15 00:50. They count as the night before, like on a printed timetable, so on Friday night the 00:15 still shows after the 23:40. You can also type 24:15.

    Saturday and Sunday are different

    Tap Weekdays, Saturday, Sunday when you add a stop, or + Add times for other days, and tick the days for each set of times. For a Friday and Saturday weekend, tick those days instead. A day with no times shows "No departures today" and the first one on the next day it runs.

    The timetable changes in the summer or school holidays

    Add a set of times with + Only between dates (optional). Between those dates it replaces the normal times for the days you ticked; before and after, the normal times run again. On a public holiday tick Today is a public holiday, or add the dates under Public holidays: those days run the Sunday times.

    Is it private? Does it work offline?

    Everything stays in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection, on a platform underground too. Add it to your home screen to open it in one tap.
