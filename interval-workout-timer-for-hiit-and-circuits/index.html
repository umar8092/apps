            Questions

            Why is my Tabata 3:50 and not 4:00?

            The rest after the last round of each set is left out, because the set ends there (the rest between sets, the cool-down or the end follows). 8 rounds of 20 seconds and 7 rests of 10 seconds make 3:50. Press See every step to check any workout step by step.

            Will it beep if my phone locks?

            A locked phone pauses web pages, so beeps cannot play until you unlock it. That is why the app keeps the screen on during a workout (switch it off under Sound and screen). The timer works from the clock, so when you come back it shows the right step and time.

            I do not hear any beeps.

            Browsers only play sound after you tap the page; pressing Start workout does that. After a reload, tap Turn sound on. On an iPhone the silent switch also mutes web pages. Press Test the sound to check the volume.

            How do I time an EMOM or AMRAP?

            EMOM (every minute on the minute): tap EMOM 10, or set Work to 1:00, Rest to 0 and Rounds to the minutes you want; it beeps at the start of each minute. AMRAP (as many rounds as possible): set Work to the total time, for example 12:00, and Rounds to 1.

            Is it private? Does it work offline?

            Your workouts and history stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection, and you can add it to your home screen.
