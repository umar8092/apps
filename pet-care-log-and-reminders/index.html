        Questions

        How often should I give flea, worming and vaccines?

        For most adult cats and dogs: flea treatment every month, worming every 3 months, and a vaccine booster every year. Puppies and kittens are often wormed every 2 weeks at first, and some products last 3 months. These are only the usual numbers, not veterinary advice: follow your vet and the packet, and change "How often" in the app to match.

        I gave it late, early or on another day. What happens?

        Tap Done today on the day you give it: the next date always counts from that day. Worming every 3 months given on 20 Jul is next due on 20 Oct. Gave it on another day? Tap Edit and set "Last done". Months are calendar months: 31 Jan plus 1 month is 28 Feb.

        What is "Just once" for?

        For a vet appointment or a one-off treatment on a set date. It shows in the list until you tap Done, then it goes away; the app offers to log it as a vet visit with its cost.

        Will it remind me?

        A web page cannot ring by itself when it is closed, so use Add the due dates to my calendar: your phone's calendar then reminds you at 9:00 on each due date. The app itself shows what is due every time you open it.

        Can my family see the same list?

        Everything is saved only on this device, so each phone has its own list. To share, use Copy care sheet or Email care sheet, or move everything with Save a backup file and Restore a backup. It works well on one shared family tablet.

        Is it private? Does it work offline?

        Your pets and records stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
