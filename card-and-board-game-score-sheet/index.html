          Questions

          How do I keep score for Pool Rummy 101?

          Pick Pool Rummy 101, add the players and tap Start game. After each deal type each player's points: 0 for the player who won the deal, 20 for a drop, 40 for a middle drop, 80 for a full count (tap the buttons under each box). Anyone on 101 or more is out, and the last player left wins. Pool Rummy 201 works the same with 25 and 50 for drops.

          Can a player who is out come back in?

          Yes, if your table allows it. Their box shows Rejoin at with the highest score of the players still in, which is the usual rule.

          Can I use it for any game?

          Yes. Choose Custom game, then choose whether the highest or lowest score wins and when the game ends. Negative points are fine: tap −/+ or type a minus sign.

          What if I typed a wrong score?

          Tap Edit next to the round in Round by round, fix it and save. Undo appears after you save or delete a round.

          Someone joins late or leaves early

          Tap + Add player during the game and choose their starting score. A player who leaves can be marked Left the table under Edit player, so they are not counted for the win.

          Is it private, and does it work offline?

          Yes. Scores are saved on this device as you type, so a locked phone or closed tab loses nothing. Your data is never sent anywhere, and after the first visit the app works with no connection.
