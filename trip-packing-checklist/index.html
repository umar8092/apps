      Questions

      How does it work out how many to pack?

      It counts the days you are away, including the day you leave and the day you come back, so 5 nights is 6 days. That gives 6 tops, 3 trousers or skirts and 7 pairs of underwear and socks (one spare). Children get one extra of each for spills. Babies get 6 nappies a day. If you tick I can wash clothes there, clothes are packed for one week at most.

      Do the numbers include what I wear?

      Yes. Wear one set on the way and pack the rest. Tap Edit on any item to change its number, or to delete it.

      What if my plans change?

      Tap Change trip or travellers, change the nights, weather or people and tap Update my list. Your ticks, your edits and your own items are kept. Things you deleted stay deleted. If an item now needs more than you packed, it is unticked so you pack the extra.

      Can I add things I always take?

      Yes. Tap + Add your own item and tick Add it to every trip I make. It is added to this list and to every new trip from now on.

      How do I pack for the trip home?

      Tap Untick everything. Every item stays on the list and all ticks are cleared, so you can check you have not left anything behind.

      Is it private?

      Yes. There is no account. Your list stays in this browser on this device, and the app works offline once it has loaded.
