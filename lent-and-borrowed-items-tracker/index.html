Questions

How do I remind someone without being rude?

Tap Remind under the item. A friendly message is ready, for example "Hi Bilal, hope you are well! Just a friendly reminder that you still have my Drill (since Sat 12 Sep). Could I have it back when you get a chance? Thank you!" Change the words if you like, then copy, share or email it.

What do "late" and "due back" mean?

If you set a Give back by date, the item shows how many days are left, Due back today on the day, and in red how many days late it is after that. "1 week" counts from the day you lent or borrowed it, so a drill lent on 12 Sep with 1 week is due back on 19 Sep. Without a date the item just shows how long it has been away.

I got it back. What now?

Tap Got it back (or Gave it back for things you borrowed). It moves to Returned with how long it was away. Tapped too soon? Use Undo, or Not back yet in the Returned list. To lend the same thing again, tap Lend again.

Is it private? Does it work offline?

Your list and photos stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Use Save a backup file to move it to a new phone. Once loaded, the app works with no connection.
