              Questions

              How are my topics spread over the days?

              Earliest exam first, and as evenly as the dates allow. Each day gets the number of topics that keeps every exam on track: topics left divided by study days left. For example 8 Maths topics over 10 study days is about one a day, and they are all done before the Maths revision day.

              What is a revision day?

              A day just before an exam with no new topics for that exam, kept to go over everything again. Each exam has its own number of revision days (0 to 14), set when you add it or with Edit exam. If there are not enough days left, the app uses fewer and tells you.

              I fell behind. What happens?

              Nothing is lost. Topics you did not tick are spread again over the study days that are left, every time you open the app. If it gets too busy, the plan says so.

              I studied ahead. Does that count?

              Yes. Tick any topic in Your exams or in the plan. Topics you tick today stay on today's list, and the rest of the plan gets lighter.

              One chapter is much bigger than the others.

              Split it into two topics, such as "Organic chemistry 1" and "Organic chemistry 2", with Add or edit topics. Each topic gets its own day.

              Is it private? Does it work offline?

              Your exams stay in this browser on this device: no account, and your data is never sent anywhere. Once loaded, the app works with no connection. Save a backup file now and then, or before changing phones.
