Questions

Does a heartbeat sound help you sleep?

Many people find a slow, steady rhythm calming, and it also covers up background noise. Results vary and there is little scientific proof for any single sound, so treat it as a comfort aid, not a treatment. If you often have trouble sleeping, talk to a doctor.

What heart rate should I choose for sleep?

For sleep, most people start with a slow resting rate of about 60 beats per minute. For a newborn, a faster 110 to 130 BPM is often used because it is closer to the rhythm a baby heard before birth. For a puppy, a slow steady rhythm of around 60 to 100 BPM is common.

Can I use a heartbeat sound for a puppy or newborn?

Many owners and parents use a heartbeat sound to help settle a puppy or a baby. Keep the volume low, place the speaker a few feet away and never inside a crib or bed, and ask your vet or paediatrician if you are unsure.

Can I play it all night?

Yes. It loops until you pause it, or you can set a sleep timer of 15, 30 or 60 minutes or 8 hours with a gentle fade-out. Phones stop web audio when the screen locks, so keep the screen on and the phone plugged in.
