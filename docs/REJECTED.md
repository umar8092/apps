# Rejected ideas (do not build)

Weak, niche or one-off. Many sites already do them, and nobody keeps them.

- Paint calculator, tile calculator, wallpaper calculator, mulch/gravel/concrete calculator (once-a-year use; already built once and called weak, see `FEEDBACK.md`)
- Tip calculator on its own (Bill Splitter already includes tip and tax)
- Random name/number pickers, coin flips, dice rollers
- Anything that is just a single formula with one result and nothing to come back to
- Trip Expense Splitter (duplicates Bill Splitter, which already says who pays whom; extend Bill Splitter instead)
- Standalone image format converter (PNG to JPG etc.): the Photo Size Reducer already saves every photo as JPG; a plain converter alone is a one-formula tool many sites offer.
- Passport photo maker with face detection and background removal: needs a heavy ML model to do well; a weak version would be worse than the Photo Size Reducer's exact-pixel crop.
- Bill due-date reminder for rent and utilities: the Subscription Tracker already lists any repeating payment with its next date and reminders; extend it instead.
- Quote or estimate maker as its own app: the Invoice Generator for Freelancers already has the form, items, tax and print; add a "Quote" document type there instead.
