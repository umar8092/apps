# Rejected ideas (do not build)

Weak, niche or one-off. Many sites already do them, and nobody keeps them.

- Paint calculator, tile calculator, wallpaper calculator, mulch/gravel/concrete calculator (once-a-year use; already built once and called weak, see `FEEDBACK.md`)
- Tip calculator on its own (Bill Splitter already includes tip and tax)
- Random name/number pickers, coin flips, dice rollers
- Anything that is just a single formula with one result and nothing to come back to
- Trip Expense Splitter (duplicates Bill Splitter, which already says who pays whom; extend Bill Splitter instead)
- Standalone image format converter (PNG to JPG etc.): the Photo Size Reducer already saves every photo as JPG; a plain converter alone is a one-formula tool many sites offer.
- Passport photo maker with face detection and background removal: needs a heavy ML model to do well; a weak version would be worse than the Photo Size Reducer's exact-pixel crop.
- Bill due-date reminder for rent and utilities: the Subscription Tracker already lists any repeating payment with its next date and reminders; extend it instead.
- Quote or estimate maker as its own app: the Invoice Generator for Freelancers already has the form, items, tax and print; add a "Quote" document type there instead.
- Road-Trip Fuel Cost Calculator (taken off the queue 2026-10-15): a single formula (distance x fuel use x price) that many sites and map apps already give, used once per trip; the Fuel Log and Car Running Cost Tracker already shows real cost per km.
- Rent and household bills splitter for housemates (considered 2026-10-17): Bill Splitter already says who pays whom for a shared bill, and Money Lent and Borrowed Tracker keeps running balances between people; a third money-sharing app would duplicate both.
- Pantry inventory with barcode scanning (considered 2026-10-17): needs a product database (no free keyless one with good coverage in Pakistan and many other countries) and camera scanning of small printed dates is unreliable; the Food and Medicine Expiry Tracker covers the real need, typing a name and a date in seconds.
- Marks percentage calculator ("I got 412 of 550, what percent?") (considered 2026-10-19): a single division that every phone calculator and many sites already do, used once per result; the Class Attendance Tracker and the CGPA planner idea cover the student needs that keep coming back.
- Family Vaccination and Checkup Record (taken off the queue 2026-10-20 after being passed over five times): opened only a few times a year, and free offline vaccination apps with each country's schedule built in already exist; a version where the parent types every due age would be weaker than those. The Emergency Medical Info app and Document Expiry Tracker already cover keeping health papers handy.
- Zakat calculator (considered 2026-10-21): worked out once a year, and many free zakat calculators already exist (banks, charities, mosques); it also needs today's gold and silver price, which has no reliable free keyless source with every currency.
- Customer credit book for shops (udhaar khata) (considered 2026-10-23): close to the Money Lent and Borrowed Tracker (per-person running balance and payments); extend that app instead if shops ask for it.
- Stock Reorder List for Small Shops as a pick on 2026-10-25 (stays in the queue): passed over for the Laundry Clothes Count Log, because shop billing apps already common in Pakistan and India (BUSY, MyBillBook) give low-stock alerts; it needs a clear angle over them before it is built.
- Keep in Touch Reminder for Family and Friends as a pick on 2026-10-25 (stays in the queue): passed over for the Pet Care Log, because several established free apps (Garden, Bond, HumanConnect) already do per-person "call every N days" reminders; it needs a clear angle, such as the local time and a polite calling window for family abroad, before it is built.
- Doctor Visit Notes and Questions for the Family as a pick on 2026-10-26 (stays in the queue): passed over for the Stock Reorder List, because several free caregiver apps (CareMobi, Caregiver Planner, CareZone) already keep visit notes and appointments.
- Homework and School Diary for Parents as a pick on 2026-10-26 (stays in the queue): passed over for the Stock Reorder List, because many schools now push their own diary apps (Satchel One, Show My Homework, Oxford School Diary); a parent-side version is still worth building for schools without one.
- Medicine refill countdown as its own app (considered 2026-10-26): the Medication Tracker already shows pills left; extend it instead.
- Baby Kick Counter for Pregnancy as a pick on 2026-10-26 (stays in the queue): passed over again for the Prepaid Class Pack Tracker, because Kicks Count and the NHS now advise knowing the baby's own pattern rather than counting to 10, and many free kick counters already exist.
- Event Budget and Vendor Payments Planner as a pick on 2026-10-26 (stays in the queue): passed over for the Prepaid Class Pack Tracker, because free wedding planners (Loverly, Zola, The Knot) already track vendor deposits, balances and due dates.
- LPG Gas Cylinder Refill Log as a pick on 2026-10-26 (stays in the queue): passed over for the Prepaid Class Pack Tracker, because Book My LPG and the government UMANG app already keep refill history in India, and the run-out estimate is the only new part.
- Baby Kick Counter for Pregnancy as a pick on 2026-10-26 15:00 slot (stays in the queue): passed over for Doctor Visit Notes and Questions for the Family, for the same reason as before (the NHS and Kicks Count advise knowing the baby's own pattern, and free kick counters are everywhere). Consider reshaping it into a "movements pattern" diary or dropping it.
- Keep in Touch Reminder for Family and Friends as a pick on 2026-10-26 (stays in the queue): lost to Doctor Visit Notes because Garden, Bond, RingPing and SoonCall already cover it for free, while the doctor-visit apps found all upload recordings to an AI service.
- Baby Kick Counter for Pregnancy, DROPPED from the queue on 2026-10-26 (20:00 slot) after being passed over four times: the UK Kicks Count charity and the NHS now advise knowing the baby's own pattern of movements rather than counting to 10, so a count-to-10 app risks false comfort, and many free kick counters (Count the Kicks and others) already exist. Only reconsider as a "movements pattern" diary with clear call-now advice.
- Tips Log for Waiters, Delivery Riders and Hairdressers as a pick on 2026-10-26 (20:00 slot, stays in the queue): passed over for Homework and School Diary for Parents because about ten free tip tracker apps already exist (Shift, TipJar, Waiter Pal, TipSee, TipKeepr), even if most keep the tax export behind a paid plan.
