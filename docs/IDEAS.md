# Idea queue

The daily job builds the FIRST unchecked idea below, then adds new vetted ideas at the bottom so there are always at least 8 waiting.
An idea only goes on this list if it solves a real everyday problem, people would keep it, and it is not already offered by every site (see `REJECTED.md`).
Each idea says who needs it, the situation, and the answer the app must give. Add the edge cases the app must handle.

Edge cases: entry and exit days both count, trips crossing the 180-day window, a trip still going on (no exit date yet), planned future trips, other rules (UK 180 days a year, custom N days in M), overlapping or mistyped dates, edit/delete with undo, backup, private. Not legal advice.
- [ ] **Food and Medicine Expiry Tracker** — A household that keeps throwing away food, or finds the cough syrup expired when a child is ill. Add an item with its expiry date (and where it is: fridge, freezer, cupboard, medicine box); see "Use soon: milk (tomorrow), yoghurt (in 3 days)", "Expired: paracetamol (2 weeks ago)", sorted by what ends first, with calendar reminders (.ics). Edge cases: "best before" vs "use by", only month and year printed (end of that month), opened items that last N days once opened, quantity, used up / thrown away (with undo, and a count of what was wasted), same item twice with different dates, copy a shopping list of what ran out, backup, private.
- [ ] **Weight Tracker with Goal Date** — Someone losing (or gaining) weight on doctor's advice or for themselves. Log weight (kg, lb or stone and lb) as often as they like; see the change this week and since the start, a 7-day average line that hides daily water swings, BMI with their height, and "at this pace you reach 75 kg around 12 Mar". Edge cases: two weigh-ins a day (keep both, average the day), missed weeks, units switched later, goal above or below the start, pace too slow to estimate (say so), pregnancy or under-18 (no BMI label), edit/delete with undo, copy for the doctor, backup, private. Different from the Home Blood Pressure Log.
- [ ] **Workout Log for the Gym** — Someone who lifts weights or trains 3-4 times a week and forgets what they lifted last time. Build workouts (Push, Pull, Legs or own) from exercises; during the session each exercise shows "Last time: 3 x 8 at 60 kg", tap to log sets (weight and reps, kg or lb, bodyweight, time for planks), rest timer, and a "new best" note. History per exercise with best set, total volume and a simple chart. Edge cases: a set typed wrong (edit/undo), plates in kg and lb, bodyweight plus extra weight, skipped exercises, two sessions a day, a session left open overnight, renaming an exercise keeps its history, copy/share a session, backup, private, works offline in a gym basement.
