    Questions

    How is the amount to save worked out?

    The app counts your payments from today (the first one is today) to the goal date: every month on the same day, every 2 weeks or every week. It then finds the smallest amount, to the cent, that reaches the goal. When the amount does not divide evenly, the last payment is a little smaller, and the app says so.

    I already put money in this month. Does it count?

    Yes. Tap Add money on the goal. It is added to what you have saved, and the next payment moves to one month (or 2 weeks, or a week) after it, so the plan does not ask you to pay twice.

    What if I take money out?

    Tap Take money out. What you have saved goes down and the plan works out the new amount you need to save from now on.

    How is interest counted?

    Only if you add it. The yearly rate is split over your payments (divided by 12 for every month, 26 for every 2 weeks, 52 for every week) and added to what you have saved before each payment, rounded to the cent. Real accounts can work a little differently, so treat interest as an estimate.

    The goal date has passed. What now?

    The goal says how much you still need. Tap Edit and choose a later date, or switch to Save a set amount to see when you will get there.

    Is it private?

    Yes. No account and no bank connection. Your goals stay in this browser on this device, and the app works offline once it has loaded.
