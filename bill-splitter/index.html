        Questions

        How do I work out who owes who?

        Choose Who owes who, add each person and type what they paid (0 if nothing). The bill is split equally and you get a list like "Sam pays Alex $50.00". Use the email buttons to send it.

        How do I split a bill when everyone ordered different amounts?

        Choose By order, add a row for each person and type what they ordered. Tax and tip are shared in proportion to each amount.

        How is the tip calculated?

        As a percentage of the bill before tax. Pick a preset or type your own.

        What does Round up do?

        Each share goes up to a whole unit. The extra goes to the tip, so everything still adds up.

        Is it private?

        Yes. Everything is worked out on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it works offline.
